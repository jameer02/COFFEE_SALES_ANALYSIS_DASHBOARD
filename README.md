# ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis & Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **coffee shop sales data** to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, popular coffee products, payment methods, time-based sales patterns, and monthly/weekly trends.

The project uses **Microsoft Excel** to perform data analysis, create pivot tables, charts, and build an interactive **Coffee Sales Dashboard**.

The goal of this project is to transform raw coffee sales data into meaningful business insights that can help a coffee shop understand its customers and improve sales performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall coffee shop sales performance.
* Identify the best-selling coffee products.
* Analyze sales by time of day.
* Compare card and cash payment methods.
* Analyze monthly sales trends.
* Analyze sales by day of the week.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
* Create visualizations to communicate important business insights.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,636 coffee transactions** with information about:

| Column        | Description                            |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `date`        | Date of the transaction                |
| `datetime`    | Date and exact time of the transaction |
| `hour_of_day` | Hour when the transaction occurred     |
| `cash_type`   | Payment method – Card or Cash          |
| `card`        | Anonymized customer/card identifier    |
| `money`       | Transaction amount                     |
| `coffee_name` | Name of the coffee product             |
| `Time_of_Day` | Morning, Afternoon, or Night           |
| `Weekday`     | Day of the week                        |
| `Month_name`  | Month of the transaction               |
| `Weekdaysort` | Numeric weekday order                  |
| `Monthsort`   | Numeric month order                    |

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 💰 Overall Performance

* **Total Revenue:** 115,431.58
* **Total Transactions:** 3,636
* **Average Transaction Value:** 31.75
* **Unique Card Customers:** 1,316

### ☕ Best-Selling Coffee

**Latte** generated the highest revenue among the coffee products, with approximately **27,866.30** in sales.

The next strongest products were:

1. Latte – 27,866.30
2. Americano with Milk – 25,269.12
3. Cappuccino – 18,034.14
4. Americano – 15,062.26

This indicates that milk-based coffee products contribute significantly to overall revenue.

### 🕐 Sales by Time of Day

Sales were relatively balanced across the three major time periods:

| Time of Day |     Sales |
| ----------- | --------: |
| Night       | 39,033.34 |
| Afternoon   | 39,018.04 |
| Morning     | 37,380.20 |

**Night** generated the highest sales, although the difference between the periods is relatively small.

### 💳 Payment Method

Card payments dominate the transactions:

| Payment Method |      Sales |
| -------------- | ---------: |
| Card           | 112,245.58 |
| Cash           |   3,186.00 |

Approximately **97% of sales revenue came through card payments**, showing a strong preference for cashless payments.

### 📅 Monthly Sales

The highest monthly sales occurred in **March**, generating approximately:

**17,036.64**

Other strong months included:

* October – 13,891.16
* February – 13,215.48
* September – 9,988.64

March was the strongest month in the dataset based on total revenue.

### 📆 Weekly Sales

The highest sales were generated on **Tuesday**, followed by Monday.

| Day       |     Sales |
| --------- | --------: |
| Tuesday   | 18,637.38 |
| Monday    | 17,925.10 |
| Friday    | 17,257.66 |
| Thursday  | 16,477.40 |
| Wednesday | 16,093.46 |
| Saturday  | 15,182.52 |
| Sunday    | 13,858.06 |

This suggests that weekday demand was stronger than weekend demand.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

### 1. Promote Popular Products

Latte and Americano with Milk generate a significant portion of sales. The business can promote these products through:

* Combo offers
* Product recommendations
* Loyalty rewards
* Limited-time discounts

### 2. Focus on High-Sales Periods

Since afternoon and night generate strong revenue, promotional campaigns can be targeted toward these periods.

### 3. Encourage Weekend Sales

Saturday and Sunday show comparatively lower sales.

The business could introduce:

* Weekend offers
* Family combos
* Buy-one-get-one promotions
* Weekend loyalty rewards

### 4. Continue Supporting Digital Payments

Card payments account for the overwhelming majority of revenue. Maintaining reliable digital payment options should therefore remain a priority.

### 5. Analyze Product Performance Regularly

Low-performing products such as Espresso could be monitored to determine whether they need:

* Better promotion
* Pricing adjustments
* Bundling
* Menu repositioning
